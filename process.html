HOW IT WORKS

My Process

A simple step-by-step process to turn your idea into a professional website.

STEP 01

🔎 Discover

First, I understand your business, your goals and what you want your website to achieve.

We discuss your business, customers, services, design preferences and required pages.

STEP 02

🎨 Plan & Design

I create the structure and visual direction of the website.

This includes the layout, sections, typography, images, buttons and overall user experience.

STEP 03

💻 Build

Once the design is ready, I build the actual website.

The website is made responsive so customers can use it on phones, tablets and computers.

STEP 04

🧪 Test

Before launching, the website is checked across different screen sizes.

I check navigation, buttons, links, contact information and general usability.

STEP 05

🚀 Launch

After everything is ready, the website can be connected to your domain and published online.

Your customers can then access your website from anywhere.

STEP 06

🔧 Support

After launch, I can help with website updates and improvements.

You can request changes to text, images, services, contact information and other website content.

What You Get
